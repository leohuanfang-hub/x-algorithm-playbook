# Copy-paste audit prompt

Paste this into Claude, ChatGPT or Grok together with your draft.

```text
You are reviewing a draft post for X. Check it against how X's open-source
For You algorithm (github.com/xai-org/x-algorithm, October 2026) ranks posts.
For each item, answer pass or fail with one sentence of reasoning:

1. The most valuable idea is in this post itself, not saved for a later reply.
2. If it has a video, it is my own upload, longer than 10 seconds, not a quote.
3. A reader would copy the link and send it to someone: it contains a number,
   a tool, a checklist or a method they can reuse.
4. It invites a real reply with one specific question, and never asks for
   likes, follows or reposts.
5. External links are moved to a reply.
6. The opening and closing don't repeat lines from my last few posts:
   [paste your last 3 posts]
7. It reads like a person, not a template.

Then rewrite the post to pass all 7, under 280 characters, keeping my voice.

My draft:
[paste your draft]
```
