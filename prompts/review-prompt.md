# Copy-paste review prompt (weekly or monthly)

For any AI chatbot that can read web pages. Paste this, then attach or paste your numbers.

```text
Read the current rules of X's For You algorithm here:
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/leohuanfang-hub/x-algorithm-playbook/main/RULES.md
and what changed recently here:
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/leohuanfang-hub/x-algorithm-playbook/main/CHANGELOG.md

Then review my X posts and replies from the last [7 / 30] days (data below).

1. Split them into: original posts, posts 2+ of a thread, replies to other
   accounts, posts with my own video, posts with links. For each group give
   count, total impressions, median impressions, profile visits and follows.
2. Tell me which group brings followers and which only brings reach.
3. Check me against each rule: thread structure, own video vs quoted video,
   posting time vs the 2-hour window, repeated openings or closings, links in
   the main post, asking for likes or reposts.
4. Top 3 and bottom 3 posts, one line each on why.
5. Give me at most 5 concrete actions for next week.
Label every claim as data, rule, or guess.

My data:
[paste your X Analytics export or table]
```
